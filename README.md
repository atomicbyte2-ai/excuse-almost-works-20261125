# The Excuse That Almost Works

> my work laptop's stuck in the lift and maintenance won't open it till tomorrow, the night gta 6 unlocks

A one-liner for the make.fun bounty "The Excuse That Almost Works": the single line a gamer texts their boss to skip work on GTA 6 launch week. `main.ts` holds the line as a constant, validates it against the sealed gate (1–140 characters, at most 20 words, a single line, no control characters, not only whitespace), then writes it with no trailing newline to `entry.txt` inside `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`) and logs the character and word counts.
